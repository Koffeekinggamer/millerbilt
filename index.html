05. What to look for

A note on Amish cabinetry

Amish cabinetry is a tradition of workshop-made work, not one universal construction standard. Many builders pair solid hardwood doors and frames with plywood cabinet boxes, and use interlocking dovetail joints in their drawers. Wood choice, fit, hardware and finish all deserve a closer look.

Oak, maple, cherry, hickory and walnut each bring their own grain and character. Finish samples are worth seeing in your own light. Ask which woods and construction details are available in the series you’re considering.

Solid wood and plywood do different jobs. Plywood is made of bonded layers; particleboard is made from wood particles. The cabinet box, joints, finish, hardware and installation work together, so a single material label never tells the whole story.
